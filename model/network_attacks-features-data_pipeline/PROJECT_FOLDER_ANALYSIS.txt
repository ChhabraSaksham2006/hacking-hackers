1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PARADIGM SHIFT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cyber defense has historically been reactive: intrusion detection systems (Snort, Suricata, Zeek)
and machine learning classifiers examine flows in isolation to declare whether an active flow is malicious
after or during its execution. In real-world enterprise infrastructure, by the time an attack signature is
detected, lateral movement has begun, credentials have leaked, or buffer exhaustion has taken down services.

This project establishes a fundamental paradigm shift from Reactive Detection to Proactive World Model Forecasting.
Rather than classifying individual packets or completed flows, the system:
  1. Observes continuous physical network telemetry aggregated over rolling 10-second temporal windows.
  2. Models the temporal dynamics and state transitions of the network over a 20-second historical lookback.
  3. Forecasts future continuous network telemetry states S_{t+K} and multi-stage attack onset y_{t+K} with
     quantifiable lead times (K in {1, 3, 5, 10} steps = 2.0s, 6.0s, 10.0s, 20.0s ahead).
  4. Emits early-warning alerts with predicted MITRE ATT&CK stages and anticipated telemetry drift deltas.

2. MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & TEMPORAL STATE SPECIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Temporal Windowing Parameters:
   - Window Duration (W): 10.0 seconds
   - Stride (Delta t): 2.0 seconds
   - Window Overlap: 80.0% ((W - Delta t) / W)
   - Lookback Sequence Length (P): 10 steps (covering 20 seconds of continuous traffic history)
   - Forecast Horizons (K): K in {1, 3, 5, 10} steps
     * K = 1  -> Lead Time = 2.0 seconds
     * K = 3  -> Lead Time = 6.0 seconds
     * K = 5  -> Lead Time = 10.0 seconds
     * K = 10 -> Lead Time = 20.0 seconds

B. Temporal State Vector S_t in R^D (Canonical D = 54 Dimensions):
   The continuous network telemetry vector S_t comprises 8 distinct functional feature groups:
   1. Volume & Density (3 dims): flow_count, total_ip_bytes, total_packets
   2. Velocity & Rates (3 dims): flow_rate, byte_rate, packet_rate
   3. Protocol Distribution (3 dims): tcp_ratio, udp_ratio, icmp_ratio
   4. TCP Handshake & Flag Dynamics (9 dims): syn_count, ack_count, rst_count, fin_count, psh_count,
      urg_count, syn_ratio, ack_ratio, rst_ratio
   5. Flow Duration & Connection Lifespans (4 dims): duration_mean, duration_std, duration_min, duration_max
   6. Packet Size & Payload Dynamics (6 dims): pkt_len_mean, pkt_len_std, pkt_len_min, pkt_len_max,
      zero_payload_ratio, payload_mean
   7. Inter-Arrival Time (IAT) Statistics (4 dims): iat_mean, iat_std, iat_min, iat_max
   8. Transport Dynamics, Window & Entropy (5 dims): win_mean, win_std, ttl_mean, ttl_std, port_entropy
   Plus First-Order Velocity Delta Features: Delta S_t = S_t - S_{t-1} across 37 base features,
   capturing rate-of-change telemetry accelerations.

C. World Model Loss Function:
   The sequence models (LSTM and Transformer World Models) optimize a joint multi-task objective:
     L_total = L_state_MSE(S_pred_{t+K}, S_{t+K}) + lambda * L_stage_CrossEntropy(Y_pred_{t+K}, Y_{t+K})
   where lambda balances physical state reconstruction against discrete kill-chain stage classification.

3. END-TO-END PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE (INGESTION -> FORECASTING -> STREAMING ALERTS)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INGESTION (scripts/download/download_official_s3.py):
   Downloads raw multi-gigabyte CSV captures of CSE-CIC-IDS2018 from AWS Open Data Registry.
   Computes SHA-256 hashes and records them in data/metadata/download_manifest.csv.

2. CLEANING & CANONICALIZATION (scripts/preprocessing/convert_raw_to_canonical_parquet.py):
   - Strips 59 repeated header rows found inside CSV dumps.
   - Eliminates 14 invalid flows with negative durations.
   - Imputes infinite rates (Flow Bytes/s, Flow Packets/s) via median training values.
   - Sorts strictly chronologically by Timestamp without shuffling.
   - Converts to fast, compressed Parquet adhering to data/metadata/canonical_schema.yaml.

3. TEMPORAL STATE AGGREGATION (scripts/preprocessing/build_temporal_states.py, src/temporal/state_aggregator.py):
   - Aggregates flows into rolling 10-second windows with 2-second stride.
   - Maps attack labels to 5-stage MITRE ATT&CK taxonomy (data/metadata/mitre_mapping.csv).
   - Produces 9 daily state parquets in data/processed/temporal_states/.

4. DATASET SEQUENCE GENERATION (src/temporal/dataset_builder.py, src/dataset_sequence.py):
   - Generates historical lookback tensors (batch, P=10, D=54) and targets for horizons K in {1, 3, 5, 10}.
   - Splits into Chronological Partitions:
     * Train: Wed-14 (FTP/SSH Brute Force), Thu-15 (DoS GoldenEye/Slowloris), Fri-16 (DoS SlowHTTPTest/Hulk),
              Wed-21 (DDoS HOIC/LOIC-UDP), Thu-22 (Web Brute Force/XSS/SQLi)
     * Val:   Fri-23 (Web Attacks)
     * Test:  Wed-28 (Infiltration Day 1), Thu-01 (Infiltration Day 2), Fri-02 (Botnet ARES C2)

5. BASELINES & EXPERIMENTATION (scripts/experiments/run_baseline_suite.py):
   - Evaluates Persistence, Majority, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, GRU, and Transformer.
   - Outputs detailed CSV metrics and reports to reports/baselines/ and reports/phase_4_5/.

6. WORLD MODEL TRAINING (src/train_world_model.py, src/train_unified_world_model.py):
   - Trains LSTM/Transformer World Models with joint continuous state regression and attack classification.
   - Saves optimal checkpoints to models/unified_world_model_best.pt and models/world_model_lstm_best.pt.

7. DEPLOYMENT & INFERENCE (src/inference_batch.py, src/inference_stream.py):
   - Batch Mode: Forensic evaluation of offline test session parquets -> results/batch_thursday_test.json.
   - Live Streaming Mode: Real-time FIFO sliding window alert generation -> results/live_stream_alerts.jsonl.

5. THE 'PERSISTENCE PARADOX' & FORENSIC RESEARCH FINDINGS (PHASE 4.5)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One of the major scientific contributions in this repository is the forensic unmasking of the 'Persistence Paradox':
  - Phenomenon: Naive persistence (predicting future state equals current state: y_{t+K} = y_t) achieves an
    apparent Macro F1 of 0.94 - 0.98 on standard test sets at K = 1.
  - Forensic Root Cause: Attack sessions in CSE-CIC-IDS2018 occur in long contiguous blocks (e.g. Botnet runs for
    720 minutes; Infiltration runs for 535 minutes). Inside an active attack block, P(y_{t+1}=1 | y_t=1) = 99.98%!
    Consequently, a naive persistence model that simply repeats the current state achieves near-perfect F1 without
    learning any predictive physics or temporal precursors.
  - Pre-Onset Evaluation: When evaluated exclusively on pure pre-onset transition windows (predicting the very first
    attack window while currently observing benign baseline y_t = 0), Persistence F1 collapses to EXACTLY 0.0000!
  - Multi-Horizon Decay: As the forecast horizon K increases from 1 step (2s) to 10 steps (20s), persistence performance
    rapidly decays, whereas sequence-aware World Models leverage early telemetry drift (packet rate spikes,
    SYN/ACK imbalances, port entropy fluctuations) to forecast attack onsets ahead of time.
  - Architectural Recommendation: Phase 4.5 reports (Reports 13-27) demonstrate the necessity of a Recurrent
    State Space Model (RSSM) with sparse latent routing to isolate subtle pre-attack precursors from dominating background.
